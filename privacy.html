Privacy Policy — Impact Garage Uploader

Last updated: November 24, 2026

What this app is

Impact Garage Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Impact Garage YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. It is not distributed to or used by anyone else.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's own channel,
  • set titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status and schedules,
  • read basic information about the owner's own channel (name, ID, statistics).

The app does not access any other user's data.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never uploaded, sold or shared with any third party. No analytics, tracking or advertising is used. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party access. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access.
