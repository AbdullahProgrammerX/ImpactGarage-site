Impact Garage Uploader

Impact Garage Uploader is a private, single-user tool used by the owner of the Impact Garage YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos, thumbnails and metadata through the YouTube Data API.

  • It runs only on the channel owner's computer.
  • It is not offered to, or used by, any other person.
  • It only accesses the owner's own YouTube channel.
